Free tools for Claude, Gemini and other AI models

Good-looking 3D from an AI, in Blender.

Blender is a free program for making 3D pictures and animation. AI models can already drive it by writing code, but they have to build every light, surface and camera angle from nothing. Blender Studio gives the AI what a photographer has: lights, materials, a camera that frames the shot, and a way to look at its own picture and fix it. {{HERO_RESULT}}

What it changes

Less to build from nothing.

Plain Blender

  • The AI writes Blender code line by line, from memory.
  • One command that this version of Blender does not have, and the step fails.
  • Lights, camera and materials all start from nothing, every time.
  • It only sees a picture if it sets up and runs a render itself.

With Blender Studio

  • 43 ready-made tools: "light this like a product shot", "make it ceramic", "frame it from three-quarters".
  • Everything is sized to the scene automatically, so a 10 cm mug and a 10 m room both come out right.
  • Every render comes back as a picture, with a warning if it is too dark or blown out.
  • A scene check catches floating objects, missing lights and subjects out of frame. Every step can be undone.

How a picture gets made

  1. BuildShapes, rocks, terrain, characters, or ready-made models.create_object · create_character
  2. FinishReal surfaces and softened edges, so objects catch the light.apply_material · add_polish
  3. StageA backdrop, a lighting rig, a framed camera and an overall mood.studio_lighting · frame_camera
  4. LookCheck the scene, render it, and study the picture.scene_check · render_preview
  5. FixChange what is wrong and render again, usually three to six rounds.undo · render_final

Blender starts by itself in the background. There is no add-on to install, no account and no API key, and it works on macOS, Windows and Linux with any AI app that supports MCP, the common way of plugging tools into AI apps.

Not tied to one AI

Gemini can drive it too.

Google Gemini was given a cut-down kit of 15 tools and asked for a toy rocket on a launch pad. It built the scene, rendered it nine times and revised between renders, with no failed steps.

It kept turning the lights up, which is why the reds came out pale. Nothing was corrected afterwards. The script that runs this fits inside Gemini's free daily allowance and is in the repository.

What is in the kit

43 tools, in plain terms.

Shapes

Basic solids, rocks, hilly ground, soft blobs, skins wrapped around stick skeletons, roughening, and cutting one shape out of another.

People

Stylised figures in toy, low-poly and blocky styles, realistic bodies, 11 poses and 6 motion clips such as walk, jump and dance.

Surfaces

24 materials including ceramic, gold, glass, wood, fabric and neon, plus photographed textures from Poly Haven.

Staging

Seven lighting rigs, a seamless studio backdrop, nine camera shots with automatic framing, and seven overall looks.

Seeing

Quick previews, a five-angle sheet for checking shape, a scene check for common mistakes, and full-quality final renders.

Motion and files

Turntables, camera moves, falling objects, draped cloth, MP4 video, and import or export for games, the web and 3D printing.

Install

Three steps, no account.

  1. Install Blender 4.2 or newer.
  2. Install uv, a small free program that fetches and runs the tools.
  3. Add the tools to your AI app.

    Claude Code:

    claude mcp add blender-studio -- uvx --from git+https://github.com/Just-Rice/blender-studio blender-studio-mcp

    Claude Desktop, Cursor and others, in the app's MCP settings file:

    {
  "mcpServers": {
    "blender-studio": {
      "command": "uvx",
      "args": ["--from", "git+https://github.com/Just-Rice/blender-studio", "blender-studio-mcp"]
    }
  }
}

Then ask for something, such as "Make a product shot of a ceramic mug on a warm backdrop."

Limits

  • No brush sculpting. Organic shapes come from noise, blobs, skins and cuts.
  • Realistic bodies have painted-on clothes and simple faces. They hold up at medium distance, not in close-up.
  • Motion clips are simple loops, not motion capture.
  • No fluid simulation.
  • Tested on macOS with Blender 5.2 only so far.

A word on safety

One of the tools lets the AI run code inside Blender, with the same access to your files as any program you run. Use it with AI apps you trust, and keep their approval prompts switched on.
